Pursehold Privacy Policy

Updated November 7, 2026

Pursehold keeps your family's money records on your phone, encrypted. This policy explains what information the app handles, what the Google services that support it process, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • No account and no bank login. Digital AI Labs doesn't run a server that stores your finances, and we can't see your data.
  • Your accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, profiles and receipts are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database.
  • No ads, and the app doesn't use your advertising ID.
  • Usage analytics are sent only if you turn them on. Crash reports start switched on during setup in most countries (off in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), and you can turn them off at any time. Neither ever includes amounts, payees, account names or notes.
  • Payments for Pro are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device: accounts and balances, transactions, categories, payees, tags and notes, budgets, bills and reminders, savings goals, pockets, money lent or borrowed, family profiles and settings. It's kept in a database encrypted with 256-bit AES (SQLCipher). The encryption key is protected by Android Keystore, in your phone's secure hardware where available. Each family profile has its own, separately encrypted data. Digital AI Labs cannot see or access any of it.

  • Receipts and photos. You choose receipts and profile pictures with Android's picker, which gives the app only the files you pick. If you take a photo, your camera app takes it and Pursehold receives only that picture. Receipts are encrypted and stored with your data.
  • Statement import. When you import a CSV or Excel bank statement, the file is read on your device. Its contents aren't uploaded anywhere.

Backups

  • Backups. A Pursehold backup is a file you save where you choose, for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app. On your first backup you decide how it's protected. With a passphrase (recommended), the file is encrypted on your phone, with a Recovery Key in a printable Recovery Kit as a fallback; we can't open it, and we can't recover a lost passphrase or Recovery Key. Without a passphrase, it's a normal file that anyone who has it can read, so keep it somewhere private. You can change this at any time in Backup & restore.
  • Android backup. Pursehold opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup and of device-to-device transfer, so its data isn't copied to your Google account. To move to a new phone, use a Pursehold backup file.

Information you choose to share

  • Exports. CSV exports, and Excel and PDF reports with Pro, are saved or shared where you choose. Exported files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Sharing. When you share a backup, export or report, Android's share sheet opens and the file goes only to the app or person you choose.
  • Emailing us. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed.

Google services used by the app

Pursehold uses the following services provided by Google. None of them receive your amounts, payees, account names, notes or receipts. Firebase Analytics is switched off when the app is installed and starts only if you turn it on during setup or in Settings. Firebase Crashlytics starts only after setup: the "Send crash reports" switch on the setup privacy step is on by default in most countries and off in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, and you can change it there or later in Settings.

Your choices

You can turn usage analytics and crash reports on or off at any time in Settings › Data & consent. Turning them off stops collection straight away, and crash reports that haven't been sent yet are deleted.

Permissions

  • Notifications for bill reminders, budget alerts and goal reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders to deliver reminders at the time you set, and to set them again after your phone restarts.
  • Biometrics for App lock and to confirm sensitive actions. Android handles the check, and the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Background work to schedule reminders and to finish longer tasks, such as a backup, if you leave the app.
  • Internet for usage analytics and crash reports if you allow them, purchases, and Google Play's review and update features. Pursehold doesn't need an internet connection to work.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to run the app, process purchases, fix problems, understand which features are useful (if you allow it), and reply when you contact us. We rely on your consent for usage analytics, and for crash reports in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland. Elsewhere we send crash reports to keep the app working reliably, and you can switch them off at any time. We rely on providing the app you asked for to process purchases. We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete any entry, account, budget, goal or profile in the app. Settings › Data & consent › Delete all data erases every profile with its encryption keys, receipts, reminders and settings, and returns the app to its first screen. It asks you to confirm with your phone's screen lock first. Uninstalling Pursehold also removes everything it stored on your device, but not backups or exports you saved elsewhere.

Usage analytics and crash reports that were already sent are kept by Google for the periods shown above, then deleted. They aren't linked to your name or email address, so we usually can't find the reports that came from your phone.

Children

Pursehold is made for adults. With Pro, parents can add profiles for their children, for example to track pocket money in Spend, Save and Give jars. That information stays on the device like everything else, and no usage analytics are sent while a child's profile is open. Pursehold is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age in your country, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Your data is encrypted in app storage that other apps can't read. You can lock Pursehold with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, give each family profile its own PIN, and hide every amount on screen with one tap. "Hide in recent apps" blurs Pursehold in the app switcher and blocks screenshots. Home-screen widgets and reminder notifications may show amounts or bill names, depending on your settings. Anyone who knows your backup passphrase or has your Recovery Kit can open your backups, so keep them private.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the UK and India, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we don't hold your finances on a server, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
