Privacy

Your money stays on your phone.

Pursehold never asks for your bank login, and we don't run a server that stores your finances. Everything you enter is kept in an encrypted database on your device, with its key held by Android's secure hardware.

Built for everyday family money

  • Quick Add

    Expenses, income and transfers in a few taps, with undo, split payments and refunds.

  • Home-screen widgets

    Add an entry in one tap, or check your balance and next bill without opening the app.

  • Import statements

    Bring in bank statements from CSV or Excel files, with duplicate checks.

  • Budgets your way

    Monthly, weekly, yearly or custom, starting on payday if you like, with rollover and alerts.

  • Bills and renewals

    Reminders for rent, utilities, subscriptions, loans and cards, plus warranties and insurance.

  • Pockets and goals

    Set money aside envelope-style, save towards goals, and track money you've lent or borrowed.

  • Reports and net worth

    Charts by category, payee and month, and net worth across bank accounts, cards, cash and wallets.

  • Every currency

    Accounts in different currencies, with light, dark and AMOLED themes.

  • 7 languages

    English, Deutsch, Español, हिन्दी, العربية, Русский and 日本語, with Arabic written right to left.

Pursehold Pro

The core app is free, with no ads. Pro adds more for the whole family.

Monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play.

  • Profiles for your partner, parents and kids, each with its own encrypted data and an optional PIN
  • Kids' allowance with Spend, Save and Give jars
  • A larger, simpler Senior style
  • Rules that sort new entries for you
  • Advanced insights
  • Excel and PDF reports with charts
  • Budget and goal widgets
  • Premium themes and app icons
