About Digital AI Labs

Small apps. Made with care.

Digital AI Labs is an independent Android studio. We build focused apps around everyday jobs, and we design them to keep your information on your device.

Meet the founder.

John Ruban Singh

Founder and developer

John Ruban Singh founded Digital AI Labs to make fast, focused Android apps that respect your privacy. John designs, builds and supports every app the studio publishes, from Smart Notes and Family Tree to Gravito.

What we believe.

  • Your data is yours

    No accounts needed, and no servers of our own. Your notes and your family history stay on your phone.

  • Offline first

    Our apps work without a connection, so they're ready wherever you are.

  • Fair and clear

    Free to download and use, with optional upgrades. In our game, every level can be won without paying.
