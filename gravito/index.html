Gravito

Change gravity. Master the flow.

A water sort puzzle with a twist. Flip gravity, turn bottles and pour sideways to sort every color.

Get it on Google PlaySee the highlights

Get the highlights.

How to play.

  1. 1

    Tap a bottle, then tap another to pour the top color into it.

  2. 2

    Pour only onto the same color, or into an empty bottle.

  3. 3

    Stuck? Flip gravity or turn a bottle to reach the color at the bottom.

  4. 4

    Gather each color into its own bottle to win, in as few moves as you can.

Fair play

Every level can be won without paying.

Hearts refill on their own, one every 20 minutes, and replaying a solved level never costs a heart. Ads are optional reward videos that play only when you tap Watch, for a hint, an undo, an extra bottle, extra moves or bonus coins. There are no banner or interstitial ads.

Made to be played your way.

  • New twists

    Episodes bring gravity flips, quarter turns, sideways gravity, mystery colors, one-way seals and valves.

  • Proven solvable

    Every one of the 520 levels has been checked by a solver.

  • Hints and undos

    Three free undos on every attempt, and hints when you need them.

  • Rankings

    Climb the all-time and weekly rankings with Google Play Games.

  • Cloud save

    With Google Play Games, your progress follows you to a new phone.

  • Plays offline

    Every level plays without a connection. Play anywhere.

  • Color-blind patterns

    Patterns make every color easy to tell apart.

  • Reduced motion

    A calmer setting for smoother, simpler animations.

  • 13 languages

    Play in the language you're most comfortable with.
