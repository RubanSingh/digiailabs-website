Frequently asked questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Smart Notes, Family Tree and Gravito work without an account or sign-up.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Smart Notes can also back up to your own Google Drive, and Family Tree saves backups to a folder you choose. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Gravito keeps a cloud save in your own Google account.

How do I remove ads?

Smart Notes and Family Tree offer Premium, which removes ads. Gravito has no banner or interstitial ads: a reward video plays only when you tap Watch.

I bought Premium on another phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy it, then open the app. Purchases are restored through Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play's refund page.

How do I delete my data?

Delete notes or trees in the app, or uninstall it. In Gravito, Settings › Reset progress erases your progress and overwrites your cloud save, and your Play Games data can be managed in the Play Games app or your Google Account.
