Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Smart Notes, Family Tree, Gravito and Pursehold work without an account or sign-up. Pursehold never asks for your bank login.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Smart Notes can also back up to your own Google Drive, and Family Tree saves backups to a folder you choose. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Gravito keeps a cloud save in your own Google account. Pursehold keeps everything in an encrypted database on your phone, and its backups are encrypted files you save where you choose.

How do I remove ads?

Smart Notes and Family Tree offer Premium, which removes ads. Gravito has no banner or interstitial ads: a reward video plays only when you tap Watch. Pursehold has no ads.

I bought Premium on another phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy it, then open the app. Purchases are restored through Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play's refund page.

How do I delete my data?

Delete notes or trees in the app, or uninstall it. In Gravito, Settings › Reset progress erases your progress and overwrites your cloud save, and your Play Games data can be managed in the Play Games app or your Google Account. In Pursehold, Settings › Data & consent › Delete all data erases everything the app stored on your phone.
