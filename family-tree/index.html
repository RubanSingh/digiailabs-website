Your family's story, on your phone.

Build your tree offline, add photos and stories, and get a notification on every relative's birthday. No account, no sign-up.

Dates

Dates the way history records them.

About 1890. Between 1900 and 1905. Before 1850. Family Tree understands exact and approximate dates, and works out each person's age and age at death.

The whole family at a glance.

Zoom and drag across generations on tablets and foldables, and tap anyone to center the tree on them.

Built for real family history.

  • Every kind of family

    Adoptive, foster and half-siblings are shown as they are, not lumped together.

  • You choose the view

    From a plain pedigree of direct ancestors to descendants, cousins and great-uncles.

  • Find errors

    When something doesn't add up, Family Tree points you to it.

  • GEDCOM 5.5.1

    Open a .ged file from another genealogy program and carry on where you left off.

  • Share and update

    Send your tree to relatives. They add what they know, and you choose which changes to keep.

  • Photos and files

    Attach photos, documents, audio and video, and crop images. On Android 13 and later, the app only gets what you choose.

  • Big trees

    Large trees open and save in the background, so you can keep working.

  • Light and dark

    Switch between light and dark, and choose from 20 color themes with Premium.

  • 31 languages

    Including Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Kurdish, written right to left.
