Read Mode

Read your notes like a book.

Turn any note into a book with a realistic page turn. Photos and videos appear within your story, and voice memos play right on the page.

Your list and your note, side by side.

On tablets and foldables, your list and the open note sit side by side. Keyboard shortcuts help you write faster.

And so much more.

  • Rich text

    Bold, italic, underline, highlight, colors, bullets and numbered lists.

  • Sketches

    Draw with a pen, pencil or marker in a full palette of colors.

  • Search

    Search all your notes at once, and jump between notes, checklists, reminders and pinned notes.

  • Calendar view

    See your notes and reminders day by day.

  • Categories

    Sort notes into categories, or create your own. Pin what matters to the top.

  • Trash with Undo

    Swipe to delete, then restore anything from the Trash.

  • Export and share

    Share notes as text, an image or a PDF.

  • Save from any app

    Send text from your browser or any app straight into a note.

  • 27 languages

    From English and Hindi to Japanese, Tamil and Turkish.
